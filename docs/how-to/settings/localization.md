Choose the language and display formats you want to use in Navet.

![Localization settings with language, time format, and temperature unit.](/docs/how-to/settings/localization.webp)

## Change the language

1. Open **Settings → Language & units**.
2. Choose **Language**.
3. Select an available translation.

Navigation, settings, dialogs, and supported card copy update to the selected language. Provider
supplied names can remain in their original language.

## Change time format

Choose:

- **12-hour** for times with AM and PM.
- **24-hour** for the twenty-four-hour clock.

## Change temperature units

Choose **Celsius** or **Fahrenheit**. Navet presents temperatures in the selected
unit where conversion is supported.

## If some text does not change

Entity names, media titles, notification messages, and automation descriptions can come directly
from the provider. Change those at the source when required.
