The Rooms workspace controls how rooms are presented in Navet and shows when an operation will also
change a connected provider.

![The Rooms workspace with Home structure, room details, and devices identified.](/docs/how-to/rooms/rooms-workspace.webp)

## Open the Rooms workspace

1. Open Home.
2. Open room navigation.
3. Choose **Customize**, then **Edit rooms** when required.

Use **Done editing** to return to normal room browsing.

## Create a group

1. Choose **Add group**.
2. Enter a household name such as **Upstairs** or **Outside**.
3. Choose **Create group**.

## Add or organize rooms

- Choose **Add room** to create an ungrouped room.
- Choose **Add room to group** to place it immediately.
- Move a room earlier or later to change its navigation order.
- Mark a room as a favorite to keep it prominent.
- Turn off **Show in room navigation** to keep the room available without listing it.

![The Rooms workspace with grouped rooms and the selected room's devices.](/docs/how-to/rooms/organized-room-navigation.webp)

## Review and save

Changes can remain pending while you organize several rooms.

1. Choose **Review changes**.
2. Read the **In Navet** and **In connected systems** summaries.
3. Choose **Save changes**.

If a provider does not support an operation, Navet keeps the supported local organization and
explains the limitation.

## If rooms already come from your provider

Use provider-backed rooms as the starting point. Navet groups, favorites, visibility, and
appearance help you organize daily navigation.
