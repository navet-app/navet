## Browse by task

  dashboards.
- [Rooms](/guide/rooms/) — organize rooms, devices, groups, and room appearance.
- [Wall displays](/guide/wall-displays/) — configure kiosk behavior, keep-awake, and low-power

- [Everyday control](/guide/everyday-control/) — lights, climate, media, security, energy,
  household chores, shopping and to-do lists, calendars, history, automations, notifications, and widgets.

  extensions.
- [Troubleshooting](/guide/troubleshooting/) — recover cameras, connections, missing entities,
  synchronization, and kiosk access.

## Before changing provider data

Some room and device operations also change your connected smart-home platform. Read the review
step before saving those changes.

Provider capability coverage varies. Check the [capability matrix](/integrations/) when a section
or action is unavailable.
